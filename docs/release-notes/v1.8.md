## Fixed

- **PierCam could open the wrong camera** — including someone's guide camera, in the middle of their guiding. It remembered the camera by a slot number the ZWO driver hands out in plug-in order, so after a re-plug or a reboot that slot could be a different camera. PierCam now locks to your camera by model and serial number:
  - it never opens any other camera on its own — not at launch, and not when it reconnects after a USB dropout;
  - if your camera is missing at launch, it shows **WAITING FOR** the camera and picks it up as soon as it appears;
  - choosing a different camera by hand asks before switching;
  - with more than one camera plugged in and none chosen yet, it waits for you to pick.

  Your existing setup is locked to the camera that recorded your recent nights. Diagnostics shows which one.
- **The target marker would not calibrate on some cameras, even on a clear night**, and blamed cloud. Seen on a wide-field ASI676MC tilted away from the pier:
  - lights and reflections on the equipment were being taken for stars, so the calibration had nothing real to match;
  - the first, quick search could miss when the lens is mounted slightly off-centre, leaving a ten-minute search that ran out of time;
  - frames from recorded nights were picked evenly across the night, so with the moon up most of them were washed out. It now picks the darkest frames, after astronomical dark.

  A clear frame now calibrates in about half a minute.

## Upgrading

Run the new installer over 1.7 — your settings and timelapse library are kept. If the target marker had given up on your recorded nights, it looks at them once more with the new search.

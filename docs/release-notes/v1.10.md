## Added

- **Sky overlay.** Labels for the sky itself on the pier cam view, placed from the target marker's calibration — so the marker needs to be on and calibrated. Config → 07 / SKY → **SKY OVERLAY**, off by default.
  - Constellation figures, drawn as curves that follow the fisheye, and constellation names.
  - Named bright stars, Messier objects with a symbol for each kind (galaxy, star cluster, nebula), the planets, the Moon, and N / E / S / W.
  - Choose which of those to show, and how many with the **DENSITY** slider, from SPARSE to EVERYTHING.
  - **ALSO BURN IT INTO RECORDINGS** is separate from the marker's own setting, so either can be live-view only while the other is burned in. With **KEEP A CLEAN COPY TOO**, the clean video stays clean of both.
  - Steady: what is shown follows fixed rules, so objects only appear as they rise or come into view and only leave as they set or go out of it, and each label keeps its side.
  - Hidden whenever the night sky isn't in view: when a connected roof file says closed, in daylight and bright twilight, and whenever the bright stars aren't where they should be — a closed roof, cloud or a covered lens. Works without a roof file.
  - Kept inside the lens's picture: nothing is drawn in the black corners outside it.
  - Light on the machine: the overlay is drawn in the background every few seconds and only blended into each frame.
- **Sky area.** Outline the open sky on the live view with the same editor as the blur mask (**DRAW SKY AREA**), and the overlay stays inside it — nothing marked behind a wall or a roof. It turns with the frame if you rotate or flip the camera.

## Changed

- The recording exposure setting **TWILIGHT RAMP** is now called **AUTO-EXPOSURE WHILE RECORDING**. With it on, the sub length and gain sliders are greyed out, since it sets them.

## Fixed

- **Recordings flickered with auto-exposure on.** It steered on the brightness of the whole frame, which on an all-sky camera can jump between the sky and the dark surround as a telescope moves, and it corrected even the smallest difference — so exposure hunted from frame to frame. It now steers on the sky alone and leaves differences under 3% alone.
- **A recording could start very green and dark for most of an hour** when the exposure was set longer than auto-exposure's maximum: the first frame was taken at the long exposure and the next ones much shorter. A recording now starts inside auto-exposure's limits.

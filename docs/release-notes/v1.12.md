## Fixed

- **"Frame failed" over and over with auto-exposure on (new in 1.11).** The new on-the-spot metering changed the gain for its quick test shots but left the camera on its old exposure, so the test shots ran far longer than PierCam waited for them, and from then on every frame failed. Metering now sets the exposure for every test shot, and every capture now makes sure the camera really is on the exposure it is about to wait for. Tested on a real ASI676MC: metering runs, every test shot is captured, and frames are recorded on schedule.
- **Switching auto-exposure off during a session left the camera on auto-exposure's last gain** rather than going back to the gain you set. The camera is now always put on the gain the frame should have.
- The status line could keep saying **"Metering the sky…"** after metering had finished.

## Upgrading

Run the new installer over 1.11 — your settings and timelapse library are kept. If you switched auto-exposure off to get around the frame failures, you can switch it back on.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/YoruDev-Ryland/PierCam/compare/v1.11...v1.12

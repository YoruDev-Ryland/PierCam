## Added

- **Blur out part of the frame.** For a name written on the pier — or anything else that should not be in a shared timelapse. Config → Image tuning → **Edit mask** opens the live view: click to place points, drag one to move it, right-click one to remove it, and add as many separate areas as you need. The blur appears as you draw, so you see exactly what will be recorded.
  - The edge fades rather than cutting, so it reads as out of focus rather than as a patch.
  - It is burned into the recording itself, so it only happens when **Blur out masked areas** is switched on.
  - The editor's toolbar can be dragged anywhere in the frame, and stays where you put it.
- **Rotate and flip the frame.** Quarter turns, mirror and flip, for a camera that could only be mounted sideways or upside down. The target marker's calibration is carried across exactly — no waiting for another clear night — and a blur mask moves with the thing it is hiding.
- **Live view rate.** How often the live view updates, 10 a second by default. Fewer frames is less CPU.
- **Camera was moved.** Tell PierCam the camera has been re-aimed, and it sets aside every night recorded before, since those show the sky from the old pointing.

## Fixed

- **PierCam used far more CPU than it should with a large camera.** The live view cost grew with the sensor, so a 12.6 MP camera did six times the work of a 2 MP one to show a sky that changes over minutes. The live view is now held to a fixed amount of work whatever the camera, so a large sensor costs no more than a small one.
- **The target marker could spend a long time on a calibration it could never find**, and start again on every launch. It now:
  - keeps a calibration once found — a pier camera does not move — instead of re-checking it every night and discarding it on a cloudy one;
  - only learns from nights recorded with the same camera and frame, since the camera was last moved;
  - gives a night three minutes to solve before judging it too cloudy, and tries at most the two most recent;
  - does not repeat a search that already failed on the same nights;
  - only learns from the live sky once it is astronomically dark.
- The library's Refresh now notices a night whose details have been edited on disk, instead of showing the old ones until PierCam is restarted.

## Upgrading

Run the new installer over 1.6 — your settings and timelapse library are kept. If you have moved or re-aimed your camera at any point, press **Camera was moved** in the Target Marker section once.

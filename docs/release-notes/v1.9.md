## Fixed

- **A dark sky came out green on some cameras.** Seen on an ASI676MC after astronomical dark; moonlit and twilight skies looked fine. PierCam balances colour so the sky background comes out neutral, but it measured the whole frame. With an all-sky lens on a square sensor, most of the frame is the dark surround, the walls and the equipment, so it balanced those and left the sky green.
  - The colour balance is now measured on the sky itself.
  - The dark parts of the frame — walls, surround, equipment — are kept neutral too, instead of turning magenta when the sky is corrected.
  - Frames with nothing dark in them, such as fog, a closed roof or a sky that fills the frame, are balanced as before.

  Brightness, exposure and the stretch are unchanged. **Neutralise LP colour** in Image tuning still switches the correction off if you prefer the sky's natural tint.

## Added

- **Roof status from a safety monitor.** Under **ROOF STATUS FROM** in the roof settings, choose where PierCam learns whether the roof is open:
  - **Roof status file** — as before, and still the default.
  - **Alpaca safety monitor** — asked directly over the network, such as StarFront's at `https://alpaca-api.tx.starfront.space`. **USE ASCOM SETUP** fills in the server and device from the Alpaca driver you made with ASCOM Driver Connect (it happens on its own the first time you pick Alpaca), and **LIST DEVICES** offers the server's safety monitors by name — "Building 2" and so on.
  - **N.I.N.A.'s safety monitor** — whatever safety monitor N.I.N.A. is connected to, through its Advanced API.

  Safe counts as open and unsafe as closed. A monitor that can't be reached, or N.I.N.A. not connected to one, counts as unknown, and **RECORD WHEN STATUS UNKNOWN** decides as before. Everything that follows the roof — recording only while it's open, the run-on after it closes, the sky overlay — works the same whichever you choose. PierCam only ever asks; nothing on the Alpaca server or in N.I.N.A. is changed.

- **Install an update at any time.** The **Install** button is no longer greyed out while a night is recording or a session is due. It still says why now isn't ideal, and offers **INSTALL ANYWAY**: a recording is stopped and its video finished first, and PierCam starts recording again when it reopens if the night is still on. Installing on its own when idle still never interrupts a night.

## Upgrading

Run the new installer over 1.12 — your settings and timelapse library are kept. Your roof file stays selected until you choose otherwise.
